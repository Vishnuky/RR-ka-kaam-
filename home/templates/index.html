How Our System Works

A fully digital, RFID-enabled linen lifecycle management system designed to eliminate losses and improve efficiency.

1

Linen Tagging & Onboarding

New linen is tagged with RFID technology, creating a unique digital identity. Each piece is registered in the system for complete lifecycle tracking.

2

Issue & Movement Tracking

Linen distribution to departments is automatically recorded. Every movement is tracked in real-time, maintaining accurate inventory.

3

Soiled Linen Collection

Used linen arrives at our facility and is automatically scanned. Manual counting is eliminated, ensuring faster intake.

4

Hospital-Grade Washing

All linen undergoes validated washing processes compliant with infection control standards. Each cycle is digitally documented.

5

Quality Inspection

Every item is inspected for quality, hygiene, and safety standards before dispatch. Only approved batches are released.

6

Delivery & Reporting

Clean linen is delivered with complete digital records and audit logs. Full transparency and accountability ensured.

Result: Faster turnaround, zero manual errors, and complete accountability across the entire linen lifecycle.
