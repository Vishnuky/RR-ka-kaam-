Your Questions Answered

What is Laundry Express?

Laundry Express is an advanced RFID-enabled linen management system designed specifically for healthcare facilities. Our platform provides complete tracking, inventory management, and analytics for hospital laundry operations, helping you eliminate losses and improve efficiency.

How does the RFID tracking system work?

Each linen item is tagged with a unique RFID chip during onboarding. Our RFID readers automatically scan and track every movement of the item throughout its lifecycle - from distribution to collection to washing and delivery. This eliminates manual counting and provides real-time visibility.

What types of items can be tracked?

Our system can track all types of hospital linens including:

  • Bed sheets and pillowcases
  • Operating room (OT) garments and drapes
  • Curtains and uniforms
  • Towels and washcloths
  • Specialized healthcare textiles

How accurate is the system?

Our system maintains 99% accuracy in tracking and inventory management. The RFID technology eliminates manual counting errors and provides real-time, precise data on all linen movements and locations.

What happens if an RFID tag is damaged?

Our tags are designed to withstand multiple wash cycles and industrial laundry processes. If a tag becomes damaged, we provide replacement tags at nominal cost. Our system can also flag items with failed tags for manual attention and replacement.
